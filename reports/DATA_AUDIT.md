### Per-dataset governance

- **asr_codeswitched_dataset** (config `default`, split `train`; audio 16 kHz;
  parquet ~3.9 GB; card claims 34.1 h total, 2.7 s avg; Egyptian
  Arabic-English lecture/talk domain). Aggregate card metadata says MIT, but
  ~12,480 clips derive from `MohamedRashad/arabic-english-code-switching`
  whose revision/source is tagged GPL and built partly from YouTube plus a
  now-unavailable predecessor. The aggregate is **not uniformly MIT**.
  Policy: audit and provisional internal research/training allowed under
  published terms; **no distribution of raw or transformed data**; final model
  publication and license claims are blocked pending source-chain review.
- **EGYSpeak** (fields `file_name`/`transcription`; ~22.4 GB audio in 23 tar
  shards; 16 kHz mono PCM). Transcripts are machine-generated by EgypTalk-ASR-v2
  and filtered: **pseudo-labelled only**, never gold. Card says CC-BY-4.0 but
  upstream Kaggle `fadisarwat/egyptian-arabic-lines` version 5 is GPL-3.0.
  Policy: E3 disabled/not runnable until the conflict is resolved; the loader
  (`masricx.data.load_egyspeak`) fails closed on audio/full use and requires
  explicit double opt-in; metadata inspection allowed; no full audio download
  in Phase 1.
- **Casablanca Egypt** (fields audio, seg_id, transcription, gender, duration).
  Policy: **evaluation only** - never train, augment, or tune on it. Validation
  may be used only for pipeline smoke/diagnosis without model selection; test
  is the final external benchmark. No redistribution of audio or modified
  derivatives; publish only aggregate metrics and brief non-sensitive examples
  with attribution.

## Audit method notes

- Raw cleaning: NFKC, strip, collapse whitespace.
- Audit/dedupe normalization adds Latin lowercasing, removal of Arabic
  diacritics/tatweel, punctuation mapped to spaces; letters and decimal digits
  preserved in all scripts; no transliteration or dialect rewriting.
- Near-duplicate detection: per-bigram hash bucketing (candidate generation;
  buckets over 64 members are skipped as uninformative) + exact bigram-Jaccard
  threshold 0.90 (min length 10 normalized chars, max 20 pairs reported).
  Worst case strictly bounded by a global comparison budget (2,000,000
  Jaccard comparisons); NOT mathematically subquadratic in the number of
  indexed texts and NOT exhaustive (can miss high-similarity pairs whose
  shared bigrams are all ultra-common: documented false-negative
  possibility). Reported pairs are Jaccard-verified. Fully deterministic and
  idempotent (insertion-order independent enumeration; truncation facts
  reported in the JSON output).
- Number frequency is reported only in privacy-safe structural buckets
  (integer_digits_1, integer_digits_2_3, integer_digits_4_plus, decimal):
  raw numeric tokens which may be PII (e.g. phone/account numbers) are never
  serialized.
- Exact audio duplicates: SHA-256 over sampling rate + canonical
  little-endian float32 PCM bytes.
- Silence: peak amplitude <= 1e-4. Clipped: >= 0.1% of samples with
  |amplitude| >= 0.999. Mismatch: chars/s < 0.5 or > 30 (non-empty,
  positive duration). Unusually long: > 50 tokens or > 300 normalized chars.
- Reports contain counts/IDs only: no audio and no PII.
